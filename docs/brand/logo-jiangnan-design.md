## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create a completely new refined logo identity proposal for the Chinese enterprise AI agent platform "枫桥" / "feBridge". User rejected the previous plant/maple emblem. This new direction must elegantly fuse a JIANGNAN STONE ARCH BRIDGE and JIANGNAN WATER-TOWN CULTURE with CONTEMPORARY SOFTWARE BRAND DESIGN. Main mark: one compact, beautifully balanced architectural symbol formed from a graceful low ancient single-arch stone bridge and a restrained water reflection underneath. A subtle sweeping Jiangnan roof-eave gesture can be integrated into the bridge parapet silhouette, NOT a separate house or building. Use the arch opening and reflection as purposeful negative space, hinting at two sides connected across water. Construct with just 2–4 confident geometric strokes/shapes, clean proportions, gentle organic curves, and generous negative space so it remains recognizable as a small application icon. An understated Chinese scholarly aesthetic, contemporary cultural institution identity, editorial elegance and precise modern geometry. The symbol must feel like a BRIDGE FIRST, not a leaf, plant, temple, rainbow, letter A, yin-yang or travel attraction illustration. No people icons, no literal AI circuits, no circuit nodes, no dragon, no mountain landscape, no roofs in a row, no circular badge surrounding the icon, no decorative seal border, no 3D or neon. Color palette: restrained deep ink-jade #1D4643, warm off-white #F4F0E7, one very small cinnabar accent #AC5947 if tasteful. Flat solid colors and crisp vector-like edges, no textured fill or gradients in the mark. Typography: exact Chinese "枫桥" in elegant contemporary Chinese typography with an understated scholarly character, clean enough for a software brand, no illegible calligraphy; exact Latin "feBridge" as a small sophisticated secondary wordmark. Design a spacious landscape brand presentation sheet. Upper main area: large original bridge-water emblem to the left of the Chinese and Latin wordmark. Lower area separated by a thin pale line: three consistent applications of exactly the same emblem, ink-jade on ivory, pale mint on deep navy square, and white on ink-jade square. Make the arch and reflected water meaningful and distinct but simple. No extra text, slogans, invented labels or watermarks. Aim for a premium timeless logo, ancient Jiangnan spirit in a modern digital connector.
